How to interpret results & limitations

Every screenshot is compared against visit 1. Red areas highlight pixels with a large RGB change. Animations, personalized content, cookie banners, timestamps and loading variance can create false positives. This is a visual discovery demo, not statistical proof of an A/B test. Real sites may block automated browsers; scans are sequential and capped at 12 visits.
